Why the Moon?

“Mathi” means moon in Tamil. So when you said your name, I found a new reason to look up at the night sky. A selenophile is someone who loves the moon, and I guess that makes me one, Vanmathi.

Moon secrets

  • The moon makes no light of its own. It only reflects the sun, and still lights up whole nights.
  • It always shows Earth the same face. Part of it we never see, like the quiet parts of a person.
  • It drifts about 3.8 cm farther from Earth every year, yet its pull still moves the oceans.

My promises

  • I won't pressure you. Your answer is yours.
  • I won't disappear. Respect and friendship stay either way.
  • I'll cheer for your dreams, whatever they are.
  • I'll keep looking at the moon, and thinking of you kindly.
